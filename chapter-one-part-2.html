Chapter One · Part 2

The Beginning of a Journey

The First Memory · A story told one memory at a time

The moon was silent, but his heart was not. For the first time in his life, he felt a strange kind of anxiety—not born of worry, but of a smile he couldn't seem to forget. Even beneath that beautiful moonlit sky, his mind kept returning to her. He could almost see her standing beside him, smiling just as she had that afternoon, as if she were still standing beneath the sun. Perhaps, if someone had looked into his eyes that night, they might have thought he was looking at the moon. But somewhere inside his memories, she was still smiling over the sun. His night had come alive. Something unfamiliar was beginning to take shape within him, a feeling he couldn't quite explain. It was as though a new beginning had quietly entered his life without asking for permission. Somewhere in the distance, a ship was beginning to sail toward an unknown destination. And that night, his own journey began—not across an ocean, but into one. An ocean of love he had never known existed, where every beat of his heart carried him further into feelings he had yet to understand. He eventually went to his room and opened the window. The cool night air touched his face as he looked up at the moon again. Then, for no reason he could explain, he felt shy. It was a ridiculous feeling. He was alone in his room, with no one around to see him, yet he found himself smiling as though she were standing right beside him. He lowered his head for a moment, almost embarrassed by his own happiness, before looking up at the moon again. For the first time, he smiled to himself without needing a reason he could put into words. He wandered around his room, returning to the window now and then, as if the night might somehow help him understand what had happened to him that day. But all he could find was the memory of her smile. Eventually, without even realizing it, he fell asleep. He had no idea when sleep had taken him. He only knew that, for the first time in a long while, he had drifted into it without trying, without overthinking, without carrying the weight of the world into his dreams. Something about that brief encounter had made him feel unexpectedly at ease, as though, for a little while, his heart had found somewhere to rest. He didn't know that a single smile could do such a thing. The night passed, and morning arrived as it always did. The world prepared for another ordinary day, unaware that something had quietly changed within him. But for him, something had changed. Even his sleep seemed to have been a pause between two lives—the one he had always known and the one that was quietly waiting for him. He had no idea that the feeling he had carried to bed that night would begin to change everything he thought he knew. He couldn't have imagined the chapter that was about to unfold, or how much of himself he would eventually discover within it. All he knew was that, somewhere inside him, something beautiful had begun to bloom. And neither the boy nor the night could have known whether that little beginning would lead him toward a beautiful tomorrow or toward a pain he had never imagined. For now, it was only a smile. And for him, that was enough.
