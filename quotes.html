WORDS & FRAGMENTS

Quotes

Words that were never meant to be loud.

A growing collection of the words that stayed long enough to become stories.

WISHES

“Some wishes are easier to make than to live with.”

MEMORIES

“Forgetting was never the hardest part. Living with the memories was.”

THE MOON

“It was indeed foolish to touch the moon while you were standing on the earth.”

THE JOURNEY

“Seems I have farther to walk, for I believed you were the last soul I’d meet before my journey came to rest. I was wrong.”

THE BOOK

“She was a book I wrote my story in, but she chose a shiny cover to cover the pages I had written.”

THE RAIN

“My tears reached the earth before the rain did. The sky asked, “Are you okay?” I smiled… and heaven lowered itself to embrace me.”

SEASONS

“If autumn takes your leaves, I’ll become your winter and cover your emptiness with snow.”

HEAVEN & EARTH

“Tell me, was I the fool who fell in love with a girl from the heavens while I belonged to the earth?”

A HAND THAT WAITED

“Take this hand that has waited only for you, and I’ll show you a world even love has never dreamed of.”

THE BATTLE WITHIN

“I fought the world while fighting a battle within myself, yet I still fought yours beside you. It was never a distraction from my pain; it was simply the love I carried for you.”

THE VOID

“If I ever disappear into the void, it won’t be because I chose emptiness. It’ll be because I’m tired of a world that mistakes kindness for something to use.”

THE MIRROR

“Even the mirror lies if you don’t know where to look.”
