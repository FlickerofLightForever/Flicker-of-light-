WORDS & FRAGMENTS

Quotes

Words that were never meant to be loud.

The published fragments of Flicker of Light — gathered here in one place, from love and longing to loss, darkness, and the light that remains.

I

LOVE

The things we give when we love someone.

1

I built a world for us, only to realize I never asked if you wanted to exist in it.

2

Take this hand that has waited only for you, and I’ll show you a world even love has yet to dream of.

3

Even the mighty sun needs the sky to hold it through the night—so who am I, just a human, to pretend I never need someone to hold me?

4

If one had loved the way I do, I would have burned the whole world just so she could plant a flower upon its ashes.

5

Did you know?

If you had only asked,
I would have become an enemy of the world
just to stand by your side.

6

I can endure every wound this world gives me, but I was never strong enough to endure a tear in your eyes.

7

I love the flower even knowing it was never mine to begin with.

8

Was it your smile that made me fall again, or your pain that made me fall deeper—just so I could carry it for you?

9

Would you let me hold your tears for a moment? Perhaps they could purify my soul.

II

LONGING

For the ones who were close enough to touch, but never close enough to keep.

10

That hand reached for mine, yet the world kept pulling me away.

11

Did you find me while you were lost in the mist,
or was it the mist itself that guided you to me?

12

I never have to look up at the moon again,
for it came to me…
and forgot the way back to the sky.

13

Maybe loving the moon from earth was never the painful part. At least you know why you can’t have it. But loving someone who is close enough to touch, yet never yours… that kind of distance hurts more than being alone. At least when you’re alone, you know you’re alone.

14

Perhaps it was foolish to reach for the moon while knowing you could never leave the earth.

15

I know I asked the night to stay like that forever, even though it had to leave so the day could come.

16

As the star were dancing for the night in the sky and yet I was looking at the moon hoping you will look at the moon like I do.

17

one sudden call of someone got my attention, I turn over and saw you smiling over the sun

18

Did you miss me ? She asked.
He smiled, raised his hand in a quiet wave, and said, I missed you with every breath I took .. and I will until I take my last breath.

III

HEARTBREAK & LOSS

What remains when love has nowhere left to go.

19

I was her shade beneath the burning sun, yet when the snow fell and the world turned cold, she was the one who cut me down

20

When our eyes met again, the sky began to growl, as though even the heavens had seen the pain hidden behind the eyes I still loved.

21

When our eyes met again, the sky began to growl, as though even the heavens had seen the sorrow hidden behind the eyes I still loved.

22

Tell me, was she the spring where I first learned to bloom, or the autumn that changed me forever?

23

If autumn steals your leaves, I’ll become your winter, covering your emptiness with quiet snow.

24

She was a droplet of rain, destined to fall for only a moment.
I became the ripple, carrying her touch across the water long after she had become part of it.

25

I was a book that no one learned to read, while you were a pen that never learned to write.

26

I wish I could forget the sky I painted with kindness and care.

27

I waved at her one last time with a smile, knowing that the next time our paths crossed, she would no longer be the person I once knew

28

I once touched the world, and it left me nothing but an empty soul.

29

Tell me, was it your lie that hurt me the most, or the truth?

30

And I carried those broken pieces with me, until I no longer had the hands to hold her, nor the strength to hold myself

31

She asked, “Are you okay?”

Not knowing she had asked a man whose eyes searched every face in the crowd, hoping to find only one.

32

I was but a wisp of wind to her- she could neither hold on to me, nor could I make myself stay.

IV

SADNESS & SOLITUDE

For the feelings people learn to hide.

33

She asked, “Wanna dance along?”
I smiled and said, “Sorry, I don’t have any strings attached to me.”

34

Why does your smile feel beautiful and painful at the same time?”
“I don’t know… it’s simply the only way I ever learned to smile.”

35

I keep my eyes closed most of the time;
seeing the truth hurts more
when the world hides its stories
behind curtains of black and white.

36

Forgetting was never the hardest part.
Living with the memories was.

37

Tell me, was I too kind for you,
or did you never notice
that every piece I gave you
was a piece of me?

38

Have you ever seen someone who forgot how to smile, yet still smiled so no one would notice?

39

I shine brighter than any light in this vast nothingness, yet not a single light shines back at me

40

the night began to shiver in the cold silence. Every second stretched into what felt like a minute. And yet his heart continued to beat, patiently waiting for someone to warm his soul.

41

I once watched the sky shrouded in a dark veil.it was beautiful yet it also saddened me.

42

The sky was beautiful with only a single moon,
but now, even with countless stars surrounding it,
it feels sad and blurred—
because you no longer look at me
the way you used to.

43

“The world knew a thousand faces of me,
but never the one that slept beneath them all.”

44

Does it hurt a little deeper when the whole world seems to be smiling, and you’re quietly learning how to smile without feeling it?

45

How empty was I,
that even the love I gave her
couldn’t find a place to stay?

46

When the fruit is picked from the tree,
is it love that reaches for it,
or desire that cannot resist
the beauty it was never meant to keep?

47

How beautiful must his soul have been,
to keep forgiving you
for one mistake after another—
because he never let your mistakes
become all that you were
in his eyes.

V

BROKEN SOULS & INNER BATTLES

The pieces that live in the darker rooms of the heart.

48

Now, I wander in search of the fire that consumes my heart—not to escape it, but to understand why it still burns for the one it can no longer reach. For no matter how far I go, I cannot extinguish its flame.

49

Let every dagger find my heart. It will never cut as deeply as the love I carry for you. For love itself was never the wound; it was the courage to keep breathing through every one of them. To understand love is to first understand the weight of pain.

50

If you were poison,
then I suppose I found peace in the very thing others feared.

51

Let me drive deeper into the abyss.
At least there, nothing pretends.

52

His soul had been shattered into a thousand pieces,
yet somehow, every broken piece still cared
for those who broke it.

53

If you ever find the broken piece of my soul,
would you let me hold it—
just once?

54

I want to burn everything that ever hurt me to ashes.
Yet when the past comes close, my hands begin to tremble, my heart races—
and the fire I carried to destroy it suddenly forgets how to burn.

55

If I ever disappear into the void, it won’t be because I chose emptiness. It’ll be because the void never pretended to care.

56

Help me turn what remains of me into wings for her, so that even after I am gone, I can still shelter her from the storms I can no longer stand beside her through.

57

He followed a voice beyond his own soul, only to discover he was meant to save hers while slowly losing his.

58

He left the page unfinished, not the story—only the part of himself that could still tell it.

VI

NATURE, SEASONS & THE SKY

Where the world outside becomes a language for what is happening inside.

59

Sometimes I feel blessed by nature itself — the sun smiles with me, the rain feels my sorrow, the storm speaks my silence, and the night lets me rest.

60

She was like the morning dew upon a leaf, every tiny droplet shimmering like a jewel beneath the gentle light of dawn.

61

The wind whispered to the leaves,

“Have you seen my love today?”

The leaves only trembled,
their branches bowing
to a silence they could not break.

“No.”

So the wind wandered on,
from tree to tree,
asking every leaf it found.

For even after a thousand “No’s,”
the wind still believed
there had to be one leaf
waiting to whisper,

“Yes.”

62

The leaves that still cling to the tree
will always know the way home.

But the ones the wind has carried away
wander wherever the seasons lead them.

For once a leaf has fallen,
it may remember the branch it came from—
yet it can never return to it again.

63

Just because you have wings
doesn’t mean you’ll always belong to the sky.

For even the birds
return when the day grows weary.

And when you do,
the home I built for you
will still be waiting—
with its door open
and its heart unchanged.

64

Just because you have wings doesn’t mean you’ll always fly the skies.
For even the birds return when the day grows weary.
And when you do,
the home I built for you
will still be waiting—
with its door open
and its heart unchanged.

65

And the wind blows, rushing toward the endless world,
while I rush through the storm—
toward the one place
that still feels like home.

66

I watched the playful leaves dance with the wind,
while time kept passing by.
I didn’t know where to go,
or what I was supposed to feel.
So I simply stood there and watched,
as the world moved on
and my time stood still.

67

I walk through a world of snow.
One day, someone asked me,
“Don’t you feel cold?”

I smiled.
“I once fell in love.”

68

The star began to crumble.

Then the stars started falling, one by one.

No one knew why.

But that night, it almost felt as if the sky was crying.

VII

LIFE, TIME & THE ROAD

The questions that remain when there are no easy answers.

69

There comes a road where every path feels closed—
not because there is nowhere left to go,
but because every heart quietly steps away
each time you draw near.

70

A little step asked an older step,

“Where are we going?
Do you have a destination?”

“I don’t know.”

“Then why do you keep walking?”

The older step left another mark.

“I just don’t know when to stop.”

71

I rode with the waves of life,
until reality suddenly became clear—
everyone was going somewhere,
while I was only moving.

72

Don’t mistake my advice for wisdom.
I’m only speaking from a place
I once had to crawl out of.

73

How is life treating you?
“It was an autumn fall, dragged into winter, while I struggled to bloom in the mud; she walked through the grass, far away from me.”

74

When it rains, I feel like I’m touching
every part of the world that sees you.

75

Tell me, how would you feel?

There is something you want more than anything in this world, yet no matter what you do, you can never have it.

You can’t move on.

You can’t forget.

So what do you do when your heart refuses to leave the place your hands can never reach?

VIII

THE LAST LIGHT

The quietest pieces—the ones that feel like endings, but aren't always endings.

76 · A CERTAIN DAY

Death waited quietly at my door.

I asked for one more day.

Not out of fear.

But because some days are too sacred to become an ending.

Today was one of them.

77

To every unread message and unanswered call… sorry.
Life taught me lessons I was never ready for,
and somewhere along the way,
I lost the road back…
now I don’t know where to start again.

78

Just as these eyes were about to close, a small ray of light found them, and a gentle breeze whispered. Not yet.

79

Have you ever seen a fallen star?”
“Yes. I see one every day.”
