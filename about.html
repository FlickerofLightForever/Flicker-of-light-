THE WORLD BEHIND THE WORDS

About

Somewhere between the words and the silence, there is a story.

Flicker of Light is a collection of stories, thoughts, and fragments written from the places people don't always know how to describe.

Some are about love.
Some are about losing it.
Some are simply about the things we carry when nobody is looking.

The stories don't always tell you what to feel.

They leave a little room for you to find yourself in them.

“A flicker can’t hold your hand forever.
It can only show you where the light is.”

Some stories are meant to linger.
