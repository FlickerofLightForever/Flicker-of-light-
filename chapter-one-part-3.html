Chapter One · Part 3

Morning Dew

The First Memory · A story told one memory at a time

Chapter One — Part Three The wind moved gently through the trees, stirring their leaves and waking the birds. Soon, their beautiful songs began to fill the morning air. Slowly, the morning rose. A gentle breeze brushed against the leaves, shaking loose the tiny droplets of dew that had rested there through the night. Beneath the first light of dawn, each droplet shimmered like a little jewel before falling toward the ground. He woke from the sweet dream he had been having. His eyes were still heavy with sleep, but the moment he opened them, his gaze rushed toward the window, as though he had left something there the night before. He hadn't left anything. Yet, for some reason, he felt as though he had. He hurried toward the open window, where the songs of the birds welcomed him into the morning. Somewhere nearby, the river flowed along its familiar course, its gentle sound blending with the breeze and the birdsong. Together, they seemed to breathe life into the world, slowly bringing him out of his sleep. Then his eyes caught sight of the morning dew resting on a nearby leaf. The leaf was close enough for him to see every tiny droplet clearly. As the first rays of sunlight touched them, they shimmered like crystals beneath the gentle glow of dawn. He stood there, mesmerized by their beauty. And then, without knowing why, he remembered her. Perhaps it was the sunlight. Perhaps it was the way the tiny droplets shimmered beneath it. Whatever it was, the sight brought him back to that afternoon, to the girl who had smiled over the sun and unknowingly left something behind in him. For a moment, he almost forgot the dew before him. His thoughts had wandered back to her smile, and the same strange feeling that had kept him restless through the night stirred inside him once more. He couldn't understand it. Nothing had happened between them, nothing beyond a smile and a brief exchange that should have been ordinary. Yet there he stood, feeling the same unfamiliar restlessness, as though something within him had been awakened without his permission. Now he understood why he had rushed toward the window the moment he woke up, even though he had left nothing there. Perhaps he hadn't left anything behind at all. Perhaps he was simply carrying something he had never known he could find. Her smile had followed him through the night and into the morning, hiding in places he would never have expected to find it. He felt foolish. How could a single smile make him behave so strangely? Why was he standing alone by a window, looking at morning dew and thinking about someone he had barely met? He had no answers. And perhaps, for now, he didn't need any. As the sunlight continued to shimmer upon the leaves, he stood quietly by the window, caught between the beauty of the morning and the memory of a girl who had unknowingly made it feel different. He couldn't help himself. He smiled.
